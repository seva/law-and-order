- Test-structure deviation declared in CLAUDE.md rather than silently tolerated
- Maximal scope ladder S1→S15 with the universal as final level; kernel pre-adaptation recorded (identity-free disputes, no wall-clock dependence, canonical rulesets are universal-scale properties)
- Operating cycle adopted as the project's iteration engine; Definition of Success is a decidable conjunction; proof = production demonstration of expected RAROC; decidability of success is the atomicity test
