# Scope — Maximal Imaginable

Established 2026-08-21. Strategy artifact; supersedes nothing in the constitution, contextualizes Phase 2+.

**Maximal mission:** a universal, self-enforcing, content-addressed judicial layer for any organization of minds — across any substrate, any distance, any time — in which order is an installable, measurable property rather than a contested authority.

The kernel is fractal: every extension below reuses the same primitives (Signal → classify → RuleSet → identity-free Dispute → Ruling → friction/phase). Scale grows around the kernel, not inside it.

## Axis II — Functional depth

- **Legislative evolution** — LLM drafts amendments from conflict evidence; ratification via timelock; constitution evolves, execution stays deterministic.
- **Sensing field** — real-time friction map of the network; early warning of phase transitions; pre-dispute routing.
- **Self-enforcing rulings** — remedies execute through escrow/multisig/timelock; law that needs no bailiff.
- **Radical subsidiarity** — compressed local oracles at peripheral nodes resolve most disputes on the spot; the center exists only for irreducible ones; the mesh replaces the hub.
- **Deep-time legislation** — amendment cycles measured in generations; at interstellar scale, light-lag itself is the timelock.

---

## Axis III — Survivability

Maximal scope implies maximal attack surface.

- Rule-mining and Sybil resistance: legislation is content-addressed, provenance-tracked, timelocked.
- Economic unbribability: no judge, no discretion, no identity — nothing to buy.
- Constitutional immune system: `BoundaryConstraints` bind every layer, including LLM legislative proposals.
- Attractor defense: at S15 the only competitor is a rival universal — the immune system is mathematical self-consistency; a ruleset that contradicts itself cannot propagate.

## Kernel pre-adaptation

The seed's constraints, adopted as engineering hygiene, are exactly the properties universal-scale law requires:

- **Identity-free Disputes** — parties need not be human, contemporaneous, or extant (S11, S12)
- **No wall-clock dependence** — no universal *now* exists across light-cones; the determinism constraint is already relativistically correct (S13+)
- **Canonical compressed rulesets** — law transmissible as pure information, re-derivable by any recipient civilization; compute subsidization at light-years (S13+)
- **Frozen versioned rulesets** — law that outlives its authors' substrate (S12+)
