## Decisions

- Conflict = legislative visibility (rule-match/appellate), not "any pronouncement": keeps the metric content-driven and non-degenerate on chat channels; the alternative (every pronounced message conflicted) saturates live friction at 1.0 and repeats the content-blindness Phase 8 exists to cure.
- Message-count window over wall-clock: replay determinism from the event sequence alone; the no-clock constraint carries into measurement by design, not exemption.
- Settlement as annotation, not force: the monitor stores the settled flag and mutates nothing; decay emerges from window turnover + the pair's post-clear silence — keeping measurement observe-only and honest about causality (the N6 disclosure).
- Component now, feed next: measurement logic is testable gateway-free; the feed is live-behavior-adjacent and gets its own step with restart verification, per the wiring precedent.
